# Project-H.O.P.E-Privacy-Policy
​Privacy Policy
​Effective Date: October 15, 2026
​Welcome to Project H.O.P.E . We respect your privacy and are committed to ensuring a safe gaming experience. This Privacy Policy outlines how information is handled when you download, access, and play our mobile game.
​1. Information We Collect
​We prioritize your privacy by minimizing data collection:
​Local Game Data: We store your game progress, coin balances, unlocked weapon upgrades, and custom settings locally on your mobile device's internal storage.
​Personal Information: We do not collect, request, or store any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, location, or contact details).
​Important Note: Because your game progression and settings are saved locally on your device, clearing the app's cache, wiping app data, or uninstalling the app will erase your saved progress and coin counts.
​2. How We Use Local Data
​The limited data saved on your device is used exclusively for functional gameplay purposes, including:
​Restoring your previous game state, stage progress, and coin inventory.
​Remembering your preferred settings (such as audio volumes, graphics quality, and control layouts).
​Tracking which character skins and combat gear you have unlocked.
​3. Third-Party Services
​External Assets: The game may utilize standard system fonts or libraries to display text and UI elements correctly.
​No Tracking or Advertising: We do not utilize intrusive advertising networks, third-party analytics trackers, or behavioral profiling tools within the mobile app.
​4. Children's Privacy
​Our game is designed to be safe and family-friendly. Because we do not collect personal data from any user, the game is safe for players of all age groups.
​5. Updates to This Policy
​We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect new features or technical updates to the game. Any modifications will be updated directly within the app or on our official distribution channels.
